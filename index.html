What is Human Face Golden Ratio?

The Golden Ratio (φ = 1.618)

The Golden Ratio is a mathematical ratio found throughout nature, art, and architecture. In facial aesthetics, it measures the proportion between facial features.

Perfect Face Proportions

Ideal faces have features that align with φ ratio: eye spacing, nose width, lip proportions, and face length-to-width ratio.

Scientific Validation

Studies show faces closer to Golden Ratio are rated more attractive across cultures and time periods.
